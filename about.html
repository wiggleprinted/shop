Our Story & Craft

Driven by a Passion for Learning

At WigglePrinted, every toy is conceived, 3D-modeled, and printed layer-by-layer in our studio to inspire curiosity, withstand vigorous play, and ignite childhood imagination.

Precision Engineering

Crafted Layer by Layer in Eco-PLA

Unlike mass-produced injection-molded plastics that sit in warehouses for months, every WigglePrinted piece is produced with intentional care using state-of-the-art additive manufacturing.

We calibrate our 3D printers down to 0.12mm layer tolerances. This ensures movable joints glide smoothly, mechanical airlocks snap securely, and articulated figures hold dynamic poses right off the print bed.

100% Plant-Based Eco Filaments

Safe, non-toxic cornstarch-derived PLA

Our filaments are free from harmful phthalates, BPA, and heavy metals. They are odorless, food-safe grade, and 100% commercially biodegradable.

Drop-Tested Durability

Internal 25% gyroid infill patterns absorb shocks from high drops and active floor sessions, engineered to last for generations.

Inspiring Learning

Tactile puzzles, articulated mechanics, and modular building components stimulate spatial reasoning and hands-on motor skills.

Never-Broken Guarantee

Because we control the 3D printer files, if a wing, fin, or connector ever goes missing, we will print a replacement part for you.
